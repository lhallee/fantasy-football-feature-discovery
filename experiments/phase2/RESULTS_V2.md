The corrected status-filtered study selected a 248-column stable Extra Trees procedure. Discovery Spearman was 0.72226. The locked 2022-2025 stress-period Spearman was 0.76583, versus 0.64594 for prior-season points. It did not approach the declared 0.90 target.

The 248 inputs comprise 196 one-to-four-season lagged elementary performance fields, 29 cutoff metadata and missingness fields, four position flags, and 19 roster-room fields. Removing six draft-related fields reduced stress-period Spearman to 0.72176. The screen found the smallest qualifying declared procedure, not a global minimum.

The raw grouped-label null did not center at zero. Eleven discovery-only permutations averaged 0.11753. Follow-up score-label, centered-noise, antithetic, and standardized-target controls support a learner-and-feature-geometry explanation rather than a direct real-label path, but the original zero-null sentinel is recorded as failed.

The historical Week 1 membership files do not have verified publication timestamps. The result is therefore a source-proxy retrospective stress test, not a sealed prospective estimate. No Phase 2 model or prediction replaced the Phase 1 2026 catalog.
